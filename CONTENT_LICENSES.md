# Content license boundary

The root `LICENSE` is intended to license TongXuan-owned software code under MIT. It does not grant rights to third-party text, curriculum, vocabulary lists, test items, images, audio, fonts, datasets, or other materials that happen to be present in this public repository. Their source-specific licenses and permissions remain in force; repository presence does not relicense them as MIT.

For content and curriculum sources, use the source registry at [`shared/content-sources/source-registry.json`](shared/content-sources/source-registry.json) and the operating rules in [`docs/open-curriculum-source-policy.md`](docs/open-curriculum-source-policy.md). The older [`data/license-registry.json`](data/license-registry.json) continues to track application dependencies, assets, and commercialization follow-up; it is not a grant of rights.

Content marked `YELLOW`, `RED`, or `UNKNOWN` must not be ingested as publishable source content. `GREEN` still requires all source-specific conditions, attribution, share-alike terms, item-level permissions, and access restrictions to be recorded and followed. A source being open for reference does not automatically authorize copying its dataset, derivatives, or media.

AI/Codex output is a proposal, not curriculum authority. A target enters an executable curriculum pack only after evidence and provenance validation and explicit human/Architect approval. This document records project policy and is not a legal opinion or a representation that any particular use is lawful.
